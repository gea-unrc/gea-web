The Applied Electronics Group (GEA) was created in 1988 within the Department of Electricity and Electronics of the Faculty of Engineering, National University of Río Cuarto (UNRC). Professors, teaching assistants, student assistants, fellows (CONICOR, SECyT-UNRC, AADECA, Fundación Antorchas, CONICET, FOMEC, FONCyT and Agencia Córdoba Ciencia) and volunteer collaborators have been part of GEA.

GEA hosts academic projects (postgraduate, doctoral and master's theses, final degree projects, research projects) as well as technology developments carried out jointly with industry. Training of human resources is an ongoing task, through specialisation and updating courses for undergraduate and postgraduate students and for industry professionals.
