Born in San Luis, Province of San Luis, Argentina (1985).

He received the title of Electronics Technician (2002) from Colegio N° 4 Fray Luis Beltrán and the degree of Electronic Engineer with a specialisation in Digital Systems (2009) from the National University of San Luis (UNSL).

He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences at the Faculty of Engineering, UNRC. He is a CONICET fellow and a Second-grade Teaching Assistant at the Faculty of Physical-Mathematical and Natural Sciences of UNSL. He has been a member of GEA since 2010.

His technical interests are: digital electronics, embedded systems, electric vehicles, and control and conversion of renewable energy sources.
