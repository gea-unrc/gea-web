Astri Edith Andrada Tivani was born in Villa Dolores, Córdoba, Argentina, in 1989. In 2018 she received the degree of Electronic Engineer with a specialisation in Digital Systems from the National University of San Luis (UNSL), and in 2025 the title of Specialist in Embedded Systems from the same university. In 2025 she began the Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences at the National University of Río Cuarto (UNRC), with a FONCyT doctoral fellowship, where she researches non-intrusive identification of electrical loads to improve energy efficiency in industrial settings and large institutions. Since 2025 she has been a member of the Applied Electronics Group (GEA) of the Institute for Research in Energy Technologies and Advanced Materials (IITEMA) at UNRC. She currently works as a First-grade Teaching Assistant at UNRC. She has extensive experience in university teaching, with a particular interest in digital signal processing, embedded systems and artificial intelligence applied to engineering and education. She gives talks in training courses on artificial intelligence and has taken part in research projects, scientific publications and national and international conferences.
