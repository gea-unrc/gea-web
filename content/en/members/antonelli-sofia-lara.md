**Electrical engineer** and **Ph.D. student in Engineering Sciences** at the National University of Río Cuarto, with a CONICET fellowship. My research focuses on the energy efficiency of induction motors, through modelling, parameter identification and performance analysis. I also work as a teaching assistant in **Electrotechnics, Electrical Circuits and Efficient Use of Energy**. I am interested in contributing to the development of solutions that improve the efficiency of electrical systems and their application in industry.
