Juan Astrada was born in Córdoba, Argentina, in October 1980. In 1999 he received the title of Mechanical-Electrical Technician from the Adrián P. Urquia Technical Institute (ITAPU) in General Deheza. In 2009 and 2010 he received the titles of University Technician in Electronics and Electronics Engineer from the National Technological University, Villa María Regional Faculty (UTN FRVM) and San Francisco Regional Faculty (UTN FRSF), respectively. In 2020 he received the degree of Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences from the National University of Río Cuarto (UNRC). He has been a member of the Applied Electronics Group since 2015 and of the Institute for Research in Energy Technologies and Advanced Materials (IITEMA) at the National University of Río Cuarto since 2018. He currently works as Support Staff of IITEMA-UNRC for the National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET) and as a teaching assistant at UNRC.
