Guillermo Rubén Bossio is an electrical engineer from the National University of Río Cuarto and holds a Ph.D. in Engineering from the National University of La Plata. He is an associate professor at the Faculty of Engineering of UNRC and an independent researcher of CONICET. His work focuses on the modelling, control and fault diagnosis of electrical machines. He combines research with the teaching of applied mathematics and engineering, the training of researchers and technology transfer to the productive sector.
