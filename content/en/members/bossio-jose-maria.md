José Maria Bossio received the undergraduate degree of Electrical Engineer and the postgraduate degree of Master in Engineering Sciences – specialisation: Electrical Engineering, from the National University of Río Cuarto, Argentina, in 2006 and 2010, respectively; the postgraduate degree of Ph.D. in Control Systems from the National University of the South, Bahía Blanca, Argentina, in 2012, and the title of Specialist in University Teaching from the National University of La Plata in 2023. In 2001 he joined the Applied Electronics Group of the National University of Río Cuarto. He is currently an Assistant Professor at the Faculty of Engineering of UNRC and an Adjunct Researcher of CONICET. His work focuses on the modelling, control and fault diagnosis of electrical machines, the application of machine learning techniques and renewable energy generation.
