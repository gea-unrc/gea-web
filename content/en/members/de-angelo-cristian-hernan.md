Cristian H. De Angelo. He received his degree in Electrical Engineering from the National University of Río Cuarto (UNRC), Argentina, in 1999, and the degree of Ph.D. in Engineering from the National University of La Plata, Argentina, in 2004.

Since 1994 he has worked in the Applied Electronics Group, Faculty of Engineering, UNRC. He is currently an Associate Professor at UNRC and a Principal Researcher of CONICET. His research interests include electric drives, electric vehicles, energy efficiency, fault diagnosis in electrical machines, and renewable energy. He is the director of the Applied Electronics Group of UNRC and leads the research lines on Electric and Hybrid Vehicles and on Energy Efficiency.

Since 1999 he has published more than 80 articles in international journals and more than 250 full papers in national and international conferences. He has supervised 7 master's theses and 14 doctoral theses to completion.

He is the director of the Electrical Power Engineering degree programme and of the Higher Diploma in University Teaching in Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, UNRC. He currently serves as Undersecretary for Science and Technology of UNRC.
