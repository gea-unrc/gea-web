Pablo M. de la Barrera is an Electrical Engineer and Master in Engineering Sciences from UNRC, and Ph.D. in Control Systems from UNS. He currently serves as Dean of the Faculty of Engineering of UNRC. His academic career combines teaching, research and university management. Since 2015 he has been an Adjunct Researcher of CONICET, and he has carried out research and training activities at renowned institutions in Germany (TU Darmstadt, OvGU Magdeburg).

He is a Senior Member of the IEEE and has held various responsibilities in its Argentine Section. His scientific work is oriented to the modelling, control, diagnosis and fault tolerance of electrical machines and drives, with applications in electric vehicles and renewable energy generation. His career is characterised by a constant link between the training of professionals, research, outreach, and commitment to the institutional development of the Faculty of Engineering and the National University of Río Cuarto.
