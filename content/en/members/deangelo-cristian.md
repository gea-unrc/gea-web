Born in Río Cuarto, Córdoba, Argentina (1974). He holds a Mechanical-Electrical Technician degree (1993, ENET No. 1 Ambrosio Olmos), an Electrical Engineering degree (1999, UNRC) and a Ph.D. in Engineering (2004, National University of La Plata).

He has been a member of GEA since 1994. In 1999 he began as a CONICET doctoral fellow and from 2004 he worked as a postdoctoral fellow. He is currently CONICET Assistant Researcher, UNRC Assistant Professor and GEA Co-Director.
