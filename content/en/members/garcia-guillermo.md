Born in Río Cuarto, Province of Córdoba, Argentina (1954).

He received the titles of Mechanical-Electrical Technician (1973) from ENET No. 1 Ambrosio Olmos in Río Cuarto and Electronic Electrical Engineer (1981) from the National University of Córdoba, all in Argentina, and the Master's (1990) and Ph.D. (1994) in Electrical Engineering Sciences from COPPE, Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

Since 1994 he has been a member of the Department of Electricity and Electronics of the Faculty of Engineering (UNRC), and since 1998 a member of the Research Career of the National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET), Argentina.

He has been a visiting professor at WEMPEC, University of Wisconsin, USA (1997), with a Fulbright research grant; at the Department of Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communications of the University School of Industrial Technical Engineering of the University of Zaragoza, Spain (1997); and at the Department of Electrical Engineering of the University of Arkansas, USA (2003).

He has taken part as a member of juries for competitions and theses, as an evaluator of fellows, researchers, programmes and projects, institutions and degree accreditation, and on the editorial boards of major scientific journals and conferences (at different universities, CONICET, CONICOR, Agencia Córdoba Ciencia, MincyT-Córdoba, FONCyT, Incentives Programme, CONEAU, IEEE, AADECA, RPIC, among others).

He has been a member of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) since 1985 and a “Senior Member” since 2001. He is currently a member of the following IEEE societies: Industry Applications (IAS), Industrial Electronics (IES), Power Electronics (PES), Control Systems (CSS), Power Engineering (PES) and Education (ES). He has also been a member of the Argentine Association of Automatic Control (AADECA) since 1994.

His technical interests, on which his publications, developments and projects with industry are based, are: power electronics, electrical machines, their control and applications oriented to energy control and conversion in renewable energy sources (wind energy), electric and hybrid vehicles, and incipient fault diagnosis in electric drives.
