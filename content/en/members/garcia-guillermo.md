Born in Río Cuarto, Córdoba, Argentina (1954). He holds a Mechanical-Electrical Technician degree (1973, ENET No. 1 Ambrosio Olmos), an Electronic Electrical Engineering degree (1981, National University of Córdoba), and a Master's (1990) and Ph.D. (1994) in Electrical Engineering Sciences (COPPE, Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil).

Since 1994 he has been a member of the Department of Electricity and Electronics, Faculty of Engineering (UNRC), and since 1998 of the CONICET Research Career.

He has been a visiting professor at WEMPEC (University of Wisconsin, USA, 1997, Fulbright grant), the University of Zaragoza (Spain, 1997) and the Department of Electrical Engineering of the University of Arkansas (USA, 2003).

He has served as a member of juries for competitions and theses, as an evaluator of fellowships, researchers, programmes and projects, institutions and degree accreditation, and on the editorial boards of journals and conferences (CONICET, CONICOR, Agencia Córdoba Ciencia, MincyT-Córdoba, FONCyT, Incentives Programme, CONEAU, IEEE, AADECA, RPIC, among others).

He has been an IEEE member since 1985 and a Senior Member since 2001 (IAS, IES, PELS, CSS, PES and Education societies), and a member of the Argentine Automatic Control Association (AADECA) since 1994.
