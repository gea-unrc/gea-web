Born in Zapala, Neuquén, Argentina, in 1988. He received the degree of Electronic Engineer from the National University of Comahue (UNComa), Neuquén, Argentina, in 2018. He has been a member of GEA since 2018 and received his Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences from UNRC in July 2024.

He is currently a CONICET postdoctoral fellow and teaching staff at UNRC.
