Telecommunications and Electronics Engineer from the Technological University of Havana (CUJAE) and Ph.D. student in Engineering Sciences at the National University of Río Cuarto (UNRC), Argentina, where he works as a CONICET doctoral fellow and researcher of the Applied Electronics Group (GEA-IITEMA).

His research career is developed mainly in the areas of automatic control, system identification, nonlinear dynamical systems and data-driven modelling. His work includes the design of nonlinear observers and Kalman filters, state estimation and the development of control strategies applied to electric traction systems and autonomous mobility.
