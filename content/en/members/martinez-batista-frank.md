Born in Havana, Cuba, in January 1988. He graduated in Telecommunications and Electronics Engineering from the “Technological University of Havana José Antonio Echeverría”, formerly known as the “José Antonio Echeverría University City” (CUJAE), in Havana, Cuba. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences at the “National University of Río Cuarto” (UNRC), in Córdoba (Argentina), and is a lecturer at the University of Mendoza.
