Born in Villa Clara, Cuba, in 1988. He received the degree of Automation Engineer in 2024 from the Technological University of Havana “José Antonio Echeverría”, Cuba. He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences at the National University of Río Cuarto (UNRC), at the Institute for Research in Advanced Energy Technologies (IITEMA), as a CONICET doctoral fellow. Since 2026 he has worked as a lecturer at the Faculty of Engineering of UNRC.
