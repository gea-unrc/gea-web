Germán Gustavo Oggier was born in Río Ceballos, Córdoba, Argentina, in 1979. He received the titles of Electrical Engineer and Master in Engineering Science, specialisation in Electrical Engineering, from the National University of Río Cuarto, Río Cuarto, Argentina, in 2003 and 2006, respectively, and the degree of Ph.D. in Control Systems from the National University of the South, Buenos Aires, Argentina, in 2009.

He is currently an Independent Researcher of the Scientific and Technological Researcher Career of CONICET and a permanent Assistant Professor of the Department of Electricity and Electronics, Faculty of Engineering, National University of Río Cuarto. He has been a member of the Applied Electronics Group of UNRC since 1999 and is currently Co-director of the group and Director of the Research Line on Electrical Microgrids and Power Electronics Applications. He is also Deputy Director of the Institute for Research in Energy Technologies and Advanced Materials (IITEMA), Director of the Electronics Laboratory, Head of the Control Area and Deputy Director of the Renewable Energy Engineering degree programme.

His technical and research interests include: power electronics, electrical microgrids, integration of renewable energy, hybrid electrical energy storage systems and control of electronic converters.
